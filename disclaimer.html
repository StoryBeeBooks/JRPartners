3. Construction Materials Disclaimer

3.1 Natural Stone Products

Natural stone (including granite, marble, quartzite, limestone, travertine, soapstone, and other natural materials) is a product of nature and inherently varies in:

  • Color and tone: Variations in base color, hue, and intensity are normal and expected
  • Veining and patterns: Veins, swirls, and natural patterns vary significantly between slabs
  • Texture and finish: Surface characteristics may vary across the stone
  • Fissures: Natural fissures, pits, and surface irregularities are characteristics, not defects
  • Porosity: Natural stone is porous and will absorb liquids if not properly sealed
  • Mineral deposits: Naturally occurring minerals may appear as spots or inclusions

Important: Samples, photographs, and digital renderings of natural stone are representative only and cannot perfectly replicate the actual slab you will receive. These variations are inherent to natural materials and do not constitute defects or grounds for rejection.

3.2 Engineered Stone Products

Engineered stone products (quartz surfaces) are manufactured materials but may still exhibit:

  • Minor variations in color and pattern between batches
  • Differences from manufacturer samples and displays
  • Variations in gloss level and finish
  • Natural quartz veining and crystalline variations

Manufacturer warranties and care requirements apply to all engineered stone products.

3.3 Wood and Cabinetry Products

Natural wood products (including custom cabinetry, doors, and millwork) are organic materials subject to:

  • Grain variation: Wood grain patterns vary significantly between pieces
  • Color differences: Natural color variation is normal and expected
  • Expansion and contraction: Wood naturally expands and contracts with humidity changes
  • Aging and patina: Wood color evolves and darkens over time with exposure to light
  • Knots and mineral streaks: Natural characteristics that add to wood's character
  • Minor imperfections: Small natural marks are acceptable in wood products

Proper climate control and maintenance are essential for wood products. Excessive humidity or dryness can cause warping, cracking, or joint separation.

3.4 Material Selection and Approval

Customers are responsible for:

  • Inspecting and approving material selections before fabrication
  • Understanding the characteristics and limitations of chosen materials
  • Accepting natural variations inherent to materials
  • Following manufacturer care and maintenance requirements
  • Acknowledging that samples are representative, not exact matches

Once materials are approved and fabrication begins, changes or returns are not possible except in cases of manufacturing defects or significant deviations from approved selections.
